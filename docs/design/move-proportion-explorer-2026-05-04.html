Methodology note ▾
National and Texas estimates are survey-weighted means from ANES data (n≈5,200). Age 50–54 and postgrad estimates are also from ANES using the same weights; joint cell estimates where sample n<100 are extrapolated using logit-additive deltas derived from the observed marginals — not MRP. The CI around thin-cell estimates is wider and not shown here. Final platform values will use full Bayesian MRP with ACS poststratification to produce proper posterior distributions for all cells. These numbers are illustrative for design purposes only.
